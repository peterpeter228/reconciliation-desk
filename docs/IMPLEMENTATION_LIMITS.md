# Current pilot boundaries

This is a private implementation, not a production accounting service or audit.

## Input and state

- One explicit company, USD, one bank account and reviewed source semantics.
- CSV/TSV and bounded values-only XLSX. No QBB/QBW, PDF OCR, macros, formulas,
  embedded objects, encrypted workbooks or unvalidated headerless bank profile.
- Data remains in browser memory. No financial-system APIs, paid model mapping,
  analytics or raw-data storage/egress. Downloads remain on the user's device.
- Canonical IDs, source hashes and physical rows are retained. Known company,
  account and identity contradictions block acceptance.
- Coverage declarations are not independent evidence of missing bank/expense legs.
  Unknown opening/closing balances remain unknown. No whole-company close claim.

## Reconciliation

File hashes prove local byte identity, not authenticity of a bank, processor or
accounting-system export. Conclusions are conditional on the supplied documents
and declared scope; no live institution independently attests those records.

Three evidence edges are independent. Supplied clearing-only samples remain
ACTIVITY_BRIDGE/PARTIAL; amount equality or a fee range is not an identity proof.
Missing money does not prove a processor hold. Statement evidence without known
release coverage can support a hold observation, not confirm current non-release.

The bounded subset search explicitly reports candidate, member and time caps.
Whole-atom/batch association is supported. A payment with multiple settlements is
blocked until a tested explicit split adapter exists. Performance measurements
state source size, runtime and unfinished search count; no universal <500ms claim.

## Accounting outputs

- JSON, formula-safe human-review CSV and printable evidence are workpapers.
- Fee-only QBO CSV and QBD IIF serializers are Beta, not target-import tested.
- Strict proposal/approval hashes bind accounts, dates, amounts, source versions,
  evidence and rules. They detect changes, not professional reviewer identity.
- Main sample adjustments remain ineligible; the separate $900 teaching example
  is marked SYNTHETIC_DO_NOT_IMPORT in names, journal text and manifest.
- The current UI exposes the teaching export workflow, not an unreviewed one-click
  real-company adjustment path. The typed builder requires full documented facts.
- UF requires payment-to-deposit linkage; ordinary JE export does not replace it.
- Download does not mean posted. Exact re-imported posted GL verification exists
  as a tested helper; target QBO/QBD account/version validation remains absent.

## Acceptance not yet established

Rendered browser interaction/visual/accessibility acceptance, actual spreadsheet
roundtrip, real authorized customer exports, target QuickBooks import/re-import,
real payment/fulfillment, and public launch. See phase-status and dated evidence.

No commercial demand, revenue, accounting certification or recovered funds are
established by synthetic sample success. Existing source-kit pricing is a proposal.
